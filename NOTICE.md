# Notices and sources

## ga403wm-hackintosh, kexts/itlmt7925Native (BSD-3-Clause-Clear)

Copyright (c) 2026 thanhnndev and contributors. Licence text in `LICENSES/BSD-3-Clause-Clear-itlmt7925.txt`.

Adapted, not copied: the shape of `src/MtkWlanNativeIf.cpp` (interface start with a RegistrationInfo block, packet pool and
queues, the core-gate / work-queue split), the join completion sequence and the event payload layouts in
`src/MtkWlanNativeReq.cpp` (link status 0xd8, assoc 0x4e, join 0xd3, first beacon 0xd4, connect complete 0xd5), the
CoreCapture logger and fault reporter setup in `src/MtkWlanNative.cpp` and `src/corecapture_min.h`, and the idea of
generating the request-slot stubs from a dump. Every offset and slot number was re-read from the macOS 26.7.1 (25G241)
binaries; that project targets macOS 15.

## Apple

The headers in `abi/25G241/` are generated by `tools/kcabi` from the kernel collection of macOS 26.7.1: class names,
instance sizes, virtual slot order and method names of IO80211Family, IOSkywalkFamily and corecapture. They contain no
Apple code. `src/skywalk_min.h` and `src/corecapture_min.h` declare the handful of Apple factory functions this kext
calls, with their mangled names.

## MtkWlan (ISC)

`src/MtkCoreApi.h` and `src/MtkUserApi.h` are copies of the core's public headers (same author, same licence).
